Math Quest

Three sample homework problems. Three ways to play with the math.

Fraction Bridge

Build both parts on equal-sized rails. When both fit, the parts join to show how much of one whole bridge they cover.

Sample homework

Add 3/4 + 1/8. Show your work with fraction strips.

0 of 3/4
0 of 1/8

Choose a piece to start the first part.

Angle Workshop

The two known angles are laid side by side. Rotate the adjustable wedge until all three fit on the straight line.

Sample homework

Two interior angles of a triangle are 48° and 67°. Find the third angle.

Parabola Pilot

Move the entire parabola up or down. Land both roots on the two marked points on the x-axis.

Sample homework

Find k so that x² − 5x + k = 0 has roots 2 and 3.

These three levels use fixed sample problems.
